# Childhood Diarrhea — WHO-IMNCI Protocol
Source: WHO Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMNCI) 2023

## Definition
Diarrhea = 3 or more loose or watery stools in 24 hours. Bloody diarrhea (dysentery) is a separate category.

## Assessment of Dehydration
### Severe Dehydration (at least 2 of these signs):
- Lethargic or unconscious
- Sunken eyes
- Unable to drink or drinking poorly
- Skin pinch goes back very slowly (≥2 seconds)

### Some Dehydration (at least 2 of these signs):
- Restless, irritable
- Sunken eyes
- Drinks eagerly, thirsty
- Skin pinch goes back slowly

### No Dehydration:
- None of the above signs

## Triage Decision
- **URGENT REFERRAL (RED)**: Severe dehydration OR bloody diarrhea with dehydration → Start IV fluids/NG tube. Refer URGENTLY.
- **PHC VISIT (ORANGE)**: Some dehydration → Give ORS for 4 hours at health facility under observation. Zinc tablets for 14 days.
- **HOME CARE (GREEN)**: No dehydration → Give ORS after each loose stool. Zinc tablets for 14 days. Continue feeding.

## ORS Preparation (Oral Rehydration Solution)
1. Wash hands with soap
2. Pour 1 litre of clean water into a clean container
3. Add entire ORS packet, stir until dissolved
4. Give child sips frequently from cup
5. Discard remaining solution after 24 hours
6. Amount: After each loose stool — under 2 years: 50-100ml, over 2 years: 100-200ml

## Zinc Treatment
- Under 6 months: 10mg zinc tablet daily for 14 days (dissolve in milk/water)
- Over 6 months: 20mg zinc tablet daily for 14 days
- Zinc reduces duration and severity of diarrhea and prevents recurrence for 2-3 months

## Danger Signs — Return Immediately If:
- Child gets blood in stool
- Child gets drinking poorly
- Child develops high fever
- Child is not getting better in 3 days

## Prevention
- Handwashing with soap (before feeding, after toilet)
- Safe drinking water (boil or filter)
- Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months
- Measles vaccination
- Clean sanitation/latrine use
- Proper food hygiene
