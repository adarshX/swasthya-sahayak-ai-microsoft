# Lymphatic Filariasis — NVBDCP Guidelines
Source: National Vector Borne Disease Control Programme (NVBDCP) 2023

## What is Lymphatic Filariasis
Lymphatic filariasis (elephantiasis) is a mosquito-borne disease caused by parasites that block the lymphatic system, causing massive swelling of limbs and genitals.

## Key Symptoms
- **Limb swelling**: Usually legs, but can affect arms. Starts with mild swelling, becomes permanent.
- **Fever with limb swelling**: Acute episodes called Acute Dermatolymphangioadenitis (ADLA)
- **Groin swelling**: Swollen lymph nodes in groin area
- **Skin thickening**: Skin becomes hard, thick, resembling elephant skin
- **Hydrocele**: Swelling of scrotum in males

## Triage Decision
- **URGENT REFERRAL (RED)**: Acute attack (fever + pain + red hot swelling) → Needs antibiotics and pain management at hospital.
- **PHC VISIT (ORANGE)**: Chronic swelling with skin changes → Refer for grading and morbidity management.
- **HOME CARE (GREEN)**: Mild swelling without fever → Teach limb care, hygiene, elevation.

## Mass Drug Administration (MDA)
- Annual MDA in endemic districts
- DEC (Diethylcarbamazine) + Albendazole OR
- IDA (Ivermectin + DEC + Albendazole) — triple drug therapy
- ASHA role: Distribute tablets, ensure directly-observed treatment, record coverage

## Who Should NOT Take MDA
- Pregnant women
- Children under 2 years
- Severely ill persons
- During acute attack episode
- Women in first week of breastfeeding (for IDA only)

## Home-Based Limb Care (for chronic swelling)
1. **Wash** affected limb daily with soap and clean water
2. **Dry** thoroughly, especially between toes
3. **Elevate** limb at night (prop up with pillow)
4. **Exercise**: Gentle range-of-motion exercises daily
5. **Treat wounds**: Clean any cracks/wounds, apply antifungal cream
6. **Wear shoes**: To prevent foot injuries
7. **Compress**: Use elastic bandage during day if available

## Prevention
- Sleep under bed net
- Participate in MDA campaign every year
- Report mosquito breeding sites to PHC
