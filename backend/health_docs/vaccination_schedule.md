# Vaccination Schedule — National Immunization Schedule (NIS) India
Source: Universal Immunization Programme (UIP), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, 2023

## Birth Vaccines
- **BCG** (Bacillus Calmette-Guérin): Protects against tuberculosis. Given in left upper arm.
- **OPV-0** (Oral Polio Vaccine): Zero dose at birth. Given as oral drops.
- **Hepatitis B birth dose**: Within 24 hours of birth.

## 6 Weeks
- **OPV-1** (Oral Polio Vaccine first dose)
- **Pentavalent-1** (DPT + Hepatitis B + Hib): Single injection protects against 5 diseases
- **Rotavirus-1** (oral): Protects against rotavirus diarrhea
- **fIPV-1** (fractional Inactivated Polio Vaccine): injection
- **PCV-1** (Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine): Protects against pneumonia

## 10 Weeks
- **OPV-2**, **Pentavalent-2**, **Rotavirus-2**

## 14 Weeks
- **OPV-3**, **Pentavalent-3**, **Rotavirus-3**, **fIPV-2**, **PCV-2**

## 9 Months (9-12 months)
- **Measles-Rubella (MR) first dose**: Very important — measles kills more children than any other vaccine-preventable disease
- **Vitamin A first dose** (1 lakh IU)
- **PCV Booster**
- **JE-1** (Japanese Encephalitis): In endemic districts only

## 16-24 Months
- **DPT Booster-1**
- **OPV Booster**
- **Measles-Rubella (MR) second dose**
- **JE-2** (in endemic areas)
- **Vitamin A** (2 lakh IU), then every 6 months up to 5 years

## 5-6 Years
- **DPT Booster-2**

## 10 Years
- **Td (Tetanus-diphtheria)**: For adolescents

## 16 Years
- **Td**: Second adolescent dose

## Pregnant Women
- **Td-1**: Early in pregnancy (if not previously vaccinated)
- **Td-2**: 4 weeks after Td-1
- **Td Booster**: In subsequent pregnancies (if previously vaccinated)

## Key Messages for ASHA Workers
1. Check vaccination card at EVERY home visit — look for overdue vaccines
2. Measles vaccination at 9 months is CRITICAL — do not delay
3. Cold chain: vaccines must be kept cold (2-8°C). Don't use expired vaccines.
4. After vaccination: mild fever for 1-2 days is NORMAL. Give paracetamol if needed.
5. Contraindications: Do NOT vaccinate if child has high fever or is severely ill — wait until recovered.
6. BCG leaves a small scar on arm — this is NORMAL and expected
7. Record every vaccine given on the MCP card (Mother-Child Protection card)
8. Report any adverse event following immunization (AEFI) immediately to PHC

## Catch-Up Vaccination
If child missed vaccines:
- Give all due vaccines at the same visit (different injection sites)
- There is NO need to restart the schedule
- Maximum priority: Measles, Pentavalent, OPV
- Update MCP card immediately
