# Family Planning and Contraception — NHM Guidelines
Source: National Health Mission Family Planning Programme 2023

## Methods Available Through ASHA
### Spacing Methods (delay/space pregnancies):
1. **Condoms (Nirodh)**: Free supply from ASHA. Protect against STIs too.
2. **Oral Contraceptive Pills (Mala-N)**: Take daily. Free supply from PHC/ASHA.
3. **Emergency Contraceptive Pill**: Within 72 hours of unprotected intercourse. Available at PHC.
4. **IUCD (Copper-T)**: Inserted by ANM/doctor. Effective for 5-10 years.
5. **Injectable Contraceptive (Antara)**: One injection every 3 months. Available at PHC.

### Permanent Methods (for couples with completed family):
1. **Female sterilization (tubectomy)**: Minor surgery, can be done at PHC
2. **Male sterilization (vasectomy)**: Simple procedure, very safe, quick recovery

## ASHA's Role
1. Counsel eligible couples on available methods
2. Distribute condoms and OCPs during home visits
3. Motivate for IUCD/injectable at PHC
4. Accompany for sterilization procedures
5. Follow up for side effects
6. Ensure informed choice — NEVER force any method

## When to Start Contraception After Delivery
- Breastfeeding exclusively: LAM (Lactational Amenorrhea Method) for 6 months
- Not breastfeeding: Can start OCP/condoms immediately
- IUCD: Can be inserted within 48 hours of delivery (PPIUCD) or after 6 weeks
- Sterilization: Can be done along with cesarean delivery

## Side Effects Counseling
### OCP (Mala-N):
- Mild nausea, headache in first 1-2 months → usually settles
- Missed period on pill → not usually pregnant, continue
- Missed 2 pills → take both, use condom for 7 days

### IUCD:
- Heavier periods for first 3 months → normal
- Spotting between periods → usually settles
- Check string monthly → cannot feel string? Visit PHC

### Injectable:
- Irregular bleeding/spotting → very common, usually reduces over time
- Weight gain → some women experience mild increase
- Return to fertility: may take 6-12 months after stopping

## Stigma-Safe Note
- Never discuss family planning in front of in-laws unless couple consents
- Use private mode for sensitive questions about contraception
- Male involvement: Encourage husband to attend counseling
- Adolescent counseling: Handle with sensitivity, maintain confidentiality
