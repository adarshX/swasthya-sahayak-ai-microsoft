# Urinary Tract Infection (UTI) — NHM Primary Healthcare Guidelines
Source: National Health Mission (NHM) 2023

## What is UTI
Urinary tract infection is an infection in any part of the urinary system (kidneys, ureters, bladder, urethra). More common in women.

## Key Symptoms
- **Painful urination** (burning sensation while passing urine)
- **Frequent urination** (feeling need to urinate often, small amounts)
- **Fever** (if infection has spread to kidneys)
- **Lower abdominal pain** or flank pain
- **Cloudy or foul-smelling urine**
- **Blood in urine** (in some cases)
- **Back pain** (kidney involvement)

## Triage Decision
- **URGENT REFERRAL (RED)**: High fever (>39°C) + back/flank pain + vomiting → Possible kidney infection (pyelonephritis). Needs IV antibiotics.
- **PHC VISIT (ORANGE)**: Painful/frequent urination + fever → Needs urine test and antibiotics. Refer same day.
- **HOME CARE (GREEN)**: Mild painful urination only, no fever → Increase water intake, refer to PHC within 2    days.

## Special Concern: UTI in Pregnancy
- UTI in pregnancy can cause:
  - Premature labor
  - Low birth weight baby
  - Kidney infection (more common in pregnancy)
- ALWAYS refer pregnant women with UTI symptoms to PHC — do NOT advise home care
- Urine test should be done at every antenatal visit

## What ASHA Worker Should Advise
1. **Drink plenty of water** (8-10 glasses per day)
2. **Do not hold urine** — urinate when you feel the need
3. **Wipe front to back** (for women) after using toilet
4. **Urinate after sexual intercourse**
5. **Wear loose cotton underwear**
6. **Complete full course of antibiotics** (even if symptoms improve)
7. **Avoid** strong soaps/chemicals in genital area

## Prevention
- Adequate water intake
- Good toilet hygiene
- Clean toilet facilities
- Cotton underwear
- Regular handwashing
