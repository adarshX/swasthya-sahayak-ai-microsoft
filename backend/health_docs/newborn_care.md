# Newborn Care — Essential Newborn Care (ENBC) Protocol
Source: WHO Essential Newborn Care Guidelines + NHM Home-Based Newborn Care (HBNC) 2023

## Immediate Care After Birth (First Hour)
1. **Dry the baby** immediately with clean cloth
2. **Skin-to-skin contact** with mother (kangaroo care)
3. **Delayed cord clamping** (1-3 minutes after birth)
4. **Initiate breastfeeding** within 1 hour of birth
5. **Do NOT bathe** the baby for at least 24 hours
6. **Keep baby warm** — cover head with cap, wrap in warm clothes

## ASHA Home Visits Schedule (HBNC)
- Day 1: Within 24 hours of delivery (or discharge from facility)
- Day 3: Third day
- Day 7: Seventh day
- Day 14: Fourteenth day
- Day 21: Twenty-first day
- Day 28: Twenty-eighth day
- Day 42: Forty-second day

## What to Check at Each Visit
### Baby Assessment
- **Weight**: Birth weight should be recorded. Low birth weight = <2500g
- **Temperature**: Feel baby's tummy/back — should be warm. Cold feet = baby getting cold.
- **Feeding**: Is baby breastfeeding well? At least 8 times in 24 hours.
- **Breathing**: Normal = 40-60 breaths per minute. Count for 1 full minute.
- **Umbilical cord**: Keep dry, do NOT apply anything. Watch for redness/pus/swelling.
- **Skin color**: Look for jaundice (yellowish skin/eyes)
- **Activity**: Is baby active, crying well, moving all limbs?

### Mother Assessment
- Check for heavy bleeding
- Check for fever
- Check for foul-smelling discharge
- Ask about breastfeeding difficulties
- Assess mental health (sadness, not bonding with baby)

## Danger Signs in Newborn — URGENT REFERRAL
- Not feeding well / refusing to breastfeed
- Convulsions / fits
- Fast breathing (>60/min) or severe chest indrawing
- Fever (>37.5°C) or feels very cold (<35.5°C)
- Severe jaundice (yellow within first 24 hours, or reaching palms/soles)
- Umbilicus: redness spreading to skin, pus discharge
- Lethargic / floppy / not responding
- Bleeding from any site

## Breastfeeding Guidance
- **Exclusive breastfeeding** for 6 months (no water, no honey, no gripe water)
- Position: Baby's tummy facing mother's tummy. Head and body in line.
- Attachment: Wide open mouth, lower lip turned out, chin touching breast
- Frequency: On demand, at least 8 times in 24 hours
- First milk (colostrum): Yellow, thick — DO NOT discard. It is protective.

## Jaundice Assessment
- Mild: Yellow face only → Keep breastfeeding frequently, sunlight exposure. Follow up daily.
- Moderate: Yellow down to chest/abdomen → PHC Visit for bilirubin check
- Severe: Yellow reaching hands/feet, OR yellow within 24h of birth → URGENT REFERRAL

## Low Birth Weight (LBW) Baby Care
- Extra warmth: Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC) — skin-to-skin for as long as possible
- Frequent feeding: Every 2-3 hours, may need expressed breast milk with cup
- Extra monitoring: Daily weight, watch for danger signs
- Delay bathing until weight ≥2500g
