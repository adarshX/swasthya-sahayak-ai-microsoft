# Child Malnutrition — WHO-CMAM & NHM Nutrition Guidelines
Source: WHO Community-Based Management of Acute Malnutrition (CMAM) and NHM 2023

## Types of Malnutrition
### Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM)
- MUAC (Mid-Upper Arm Circumference) < 11.5 cm (for 6-59 months)
- Weight-for-height < -3 Z-score
- Bilateral pitting edema (both feet swollen)

### Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM)
- MUAC 11.5 - 12.5 cm
- Weight-for-height between -2 and -3 Z-score

### Stunting (Chronic Malnutrition)
- Height-for-age < -2 Z-score
- Long-term undernutrition, harder to reverse

## Key Signs to Look For
- **Visible wasting**: Ribs clearly visible, muscle wasting in arms/legs/buttocks
- **Bilateral edema**: Press both feet for 3 seconds — if pit remains, child has edema
- **MUAC tape**: Wrap around left upper arm midpoint
  - Green (≥13.5 cm): Normal
  - Yellow (12.5-13.5 cm): At risk
  - Orange (11.5-12.5 cm): MAM
  - Red (<11.5 cm): SAM — urgent referral

## Triage Decision
- **URGENT REFERRAL (RED)**: SAM with any medical complication (unable to eat, fever, dehydration, edema) → Refer to Nutrition Rehabilitation Centre (NRC).
- **PHC VISIT (ORANGE)**: SAM without complications → Start Ready-to-Use Therapeutic Food (RUTF). Weekly follow-up. OR MAM with any illness.
- **HOME CARE (GREEN)**: MAM without illness → Nutritional counseling. Supplementary feeding. Monthly weight monitoring.

## Feeding Advice by Age
### 0-6 months
- EXCLUSIVE breastfeeding (no water, no other food)
- On-demand feeding, at least 8 times per day

### 6-8 months
- Continue breastfeeding
- Start complementary foods: mashed dal, khichdi, mashed banana, suji halwa
- 2-3 meals + 1-2 snacks per day

### 9-11 months
- Finely chopped family food
- 3-4 meals + 1-2 snacks per day
- Add oil/ghee for calories

### 12-24 months
- Family food (avoid spicy/fried)
- 3-4 meals + 2 snacks
- Continue breastfeeding

## Vitamin A Supplementation
- 6-11 months: 1 lakh IU (100,000 IU) every 6 months
- 12-59 months: 2 lakh IU (200,000 IU) every 6 months
- Given during routine immunization visits

## Growth Monitoring
- Weigh child monthly on Anganwadi day
- Plot on growth chart (MCP card)
- Two consecutive months of no weight gain = action needed
- Declining weight = urgent counseling + PHC referral

## Ready-to-Use Therapeutic Food (RUTF)
- For SAM children without complications
- Peanut-based paste, energy-dense (500 kcal per packet)
- Given for 6-8 weeks under NRC follow-up
- Do NOT dilute or mix with water
- Child should eat small amounts throughout the day
